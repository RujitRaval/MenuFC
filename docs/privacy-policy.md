# MenuFC — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 11, 2026

MenuFC ("the app") is a macOS menu bar application that displays live football scores. This
policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: **MenuFC
does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

## What we collect

**Nothing personal.** MenuFC has no account, no login, and no sign-up. It does not ask for
your name, email, contacts, location, or any other personal information, and it contains no
advertising or third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

## How the app works

To show scores, MenuFC sends anonymous requests over HTTPS to our own server (a Cloudflare
Worker), which in turn fetches publicly available match data from a third-party football data
provider. These requests carry only what is needed to return today's scores. They do not
include any personal identifier, and we do not build a profile of you or your device.

Like virtually all internet services, our server may process standard, transient connection
information (such as an IP address) solely to deliver the response and protect the service
from abuse. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not sell or share it.

## Data stored on your Mac

MenuFC caches the most recent scores locally on your Mac (in your Application Support folder)
so it can show the last known result when you're offline. This cache stays on your device,
contains only public match data, and is removed when you delete the app.

## Children's privacy

MenuFC is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Third parties

MenuFC relies on a third-party provider for match data. The app communicates with that
provider only through our server — your device never contacts the provider directly, and no
personal information is sent to them.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes (for example, if analytics are added in a future version),
this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version
ships.
